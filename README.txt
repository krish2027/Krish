# KRISH — 3D Animator Portfolio

## Run
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open `index.html` in Chrome/Edge.
3. Internet is required for the Google Font and Three.js CDN.

## Customize
- Change your email in `index.html`.
- Replace the CV placeholder link with your PDF.
- Replace social links with your real profiles.
- Edit project names/descriptions directly in `index.html`.
- The hero astronaut, orbit and floating rocks are generated with Three.js, so no model downloads are required.

## 3D mouse interaction
Move your mouse around the hero section. The astronaut follows the cursor smoothly and the scene floats continuously.
